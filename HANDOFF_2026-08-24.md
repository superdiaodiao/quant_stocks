## 0. User's full objective

The objective is not merely to preserve the existing v4 parameters or to wait
for a year of shadow data. The intended end state is:

1. Make the 2019-2021 historical path as complete and usable as later years,
   while retaining explicit evidence for gaps that cannot be recovered.
2. Keep the historical security universe PIT-correct, including ordinary-share
   filtering, warrants/units/rights exclusion, ticker/name changes, delistings,
   and dated membership lists. A current Nasdaq list must not be projected
   backward as if it were historical truth.
3. After the input path is stable, retrain strategy selection rather than
   assuming today's fixed parameters are optimal. The training procedure should
   adapt within a policy frozen before the holdout is inspected, use walk-forward
   or nested time splits, and minimize selection overfit.
4. Use the available history efficiently: training, validation, and final
   historical holdout periods must be separated. 2019-2021 should become real
   evidence rather than being silently dropped because it is inconvenient.
5. Once a candidate passes historical data-completeness and robustness gates,
   shorten the genuine forward observation phase from the former one-year rule
   where statistically defensible. The working target is roughly 3-6 months,
   with pre-frozen evidence thresholds and no parameter changes during that
   period; elapsed time alone is not evidence.
6. If those gates pass, move toward live use as quickly as responsibly possible,
   beginning with a small-capital, staged rollout and explicit kill switches.
   Brokerage connection, order creation, and automated trading remain outside
   the current authorization and require a later explicit decision.

The immediate next milestone is therefore: close or rigorously classify the
remaining historical data gaps, freeze an anti-overfit adaptive-training
protocol, run it once, evaluate an untouched historical holdout, and only then
define the shorter forward gate. Do not optimize the observation duration by
repeatedly looking at the same holdout.
